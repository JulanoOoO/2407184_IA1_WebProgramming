Personal Development"

"Seven Cures for a Lean Purse"

When building wealth, you should start keeping at least ten percent of what you earn, then pay for everything else. For you to actually make this happen, it is important to have strict control over your expenses by knowing what is essential and what are the unnecessary desires that take up income unknowingly. Money saved should be put to work in something safe, like an investment that you trust, so it can compound and grow for continuous earnings. One should avoid get-rich schemes when investing to protect their capital and seek advice from people with proven experience. Finally, secure a home for your future and plan for retirement while continuing to improve your skills to earn.

"Five Laws of Gold"

Once you consistently save a tenth of your income and put it to use to gain profit, wealth will naturally flow towards you. And to protect this wealth, it is important to take guidance from persons who are more experienced and understand how to handle capital. Point to note: money that is invested in high-risk schemes that promise rewards that are unrealistic is highly likely to be lost.

"Take away"

Financial security is a matter of how much you can keep and put back to work; it does not depend on luck and how much you earn. By using one's savings to actively produce capital, protecting said capital from unnecessary risk, and letting it grow while you also grow and gain the skills needed for financial independence.
