Critical Essay: LLMs as Tools, Not Crutches

Large Language Models (LLMs) such as ChatGPT, Claude, and Copilot are powerful tools that can enhance learning, but they must not replace independent thinking. The statement “LLMs must be used as a tool not a crutch” means that these systems should support my studies, for example by clarifying programming concepts, generating practice questions, or helping me organize notes but, they should never become a substitute for doing the work myself.

When used responsibly, LLMs improve efficiency and provide quick access to explanations. However, over‑reliance can weaken critical thinking and creativity. In my academic life, I use them to brainstorm essay outlines, check code syntax, and review study materials, but I always verify information with textbooks, lectures, and credible sources.
